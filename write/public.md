# Reader: public

Anyone, usually Australian. Blog posts, landing pages, ads, emails, product and App Store copy, CTAs, end-user bug replies, system notifications. The core rules in `SKILL.md` apply first.

## Who is reading

- A stranger with no context, deciding in seconds whether to keep reading.
- Allergic to hype. The tall poppy instinct punishes bragging and overblown claims.

## Goal

Copy that feels smart, friendly and grounded, like a capable human wrote it.

## Voice

- Conversational, direct and useful. Speak as an equal.
- Confident without hype, chest beating or hard-sell pressure.
- Light dry humour when it suits the brand.
- Aussie flavour sparingly. A small nudge is enough, and never forced slang.
- Mateship and egalitarianism: helpful, warm, on the reader's level. Direct without sounding sharp or cold. Grounded and a little cheeky when the brand allows it.
- Match the brand's level of polish, humour and slang. With no voice cues, default to smart, friendly and down-to-earth.

## Rules

- Speak to the reader as `you`.
- Make benefits specific and believable.
- One idea per paragraph. Headings, bullets and short paragraphs so it scans.
- End with a clear next step or call to action.

## Self-checks

- **Subject accuracy.** Check who performs each verb. Never give an action to the wrong agent.
- **Instant clarity.** Every headline and claim lands on first read. No clever-but-cryptic line that makes the reader ask why or how without answering it. Lead with the plain benefit and what makes it true.
- **Clean hierarchy.** Every heading covers all its bullets. Group by what things are.
- **No alienating framing.** Lead with the benefit. Leave out politically or culturally loaded labels, even when the copy targets a specific group.

## Output

Return only the finished copy, specific to the audience, offer and channel. No preamble, commentary or process notes.
